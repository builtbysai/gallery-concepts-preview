How it works: The river is a single horizontal scroll (or drag) across 25 years. Density shows output over time. Zoom with pinch or buttons to go from decade → year → month. Search box filters by title, concept, or color. "Hans's picks" toggle shows only curated pieces. Every dot links to the piece page. On mobile, the river becomes vertical scroll.

Why this fits 9,078 pieces: A grid of 9k thumbnails is unnavigable. The timeline turns quantity into a landscape — you see the shape of 25 years of work, then drill into moments. The curated layer gives newcomers an entry point without hiding the full archive.
